My OJT experience was different from working on purely academic projects because the work had real users, real deadlines, real feedback, and real deliverables.

I learned to switch between different disciplines depending on what the team needed. PAGE 12 — MY REFLECTION PERSONAL TAKEAWAYS

What I Took Away

MORE THAN INTERFACES

Being a designer is not only about creating good-looking interfaces. It is about understanding the problem, communicating with people, working within constraints, receiving feedback, making decisions, and delivering something that actually works.

POWER OF ADAPTABILITY

One of the biggest lessons I gained was the importance of adaptability. I had to move seamlessly between UI/UX, web development, video editing, photography, branding, and motion graphics depending on what the project required.

BEYOND THE DESIGN FILE

Design becomes more valuable when I understand what happens beyond the design file. Working directly with development, content, production, and creative teams helped me see the entire process more clearly.

THE BIGGEST LESSON
"Good design is not just about what I can create. It is about how effectively I can contribute to the problem."
